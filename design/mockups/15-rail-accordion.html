Accordion nav groups

A labeled rail group with more than one item now collapses behind a toggle

What changed

<assay-rail> groups with a label and more than one item render as a collapsible section — a clickable header with a chevron, one group open at a time. The group containing the active route auto-expands on load and on navigation; otherwise the rail remembers whichever group a rider last opened, per service, in localStorage.

Applications is open above because it's this page's active section. Try the rail on the left: click MMI or Screening & KYE to open them — Applications will close, since only one group stays open at a time.

The open group carries its own tone — --a-rail-group-open, the rail navy pulled 16% toward brand teal — so header and items read as one panel rather than a brightness step. It sits deliberately between --a-rail and --a-rail-active, leaving the active item's fill and Current slice legible inside it.

A group with no label, or exactly one item, still renders flat — nothing to disclose there, so no toggle chrome appears (see Overview above the accordion groups).
