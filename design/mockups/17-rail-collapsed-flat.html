Collapsed rail — no categories

A flat nav keeps every destination, as its own icon

What you're looking at

Services with a short, flat nav — Connect, Payment, Secure Share — have no categories to stand in for, so collapsing keeps every destination and shows each item's own icon. Nothing is hidden; only the labels go. The active item keeps its fill and Current slice exactly as it does in the full rail.

A badge has no room for its count at 72px, so it degrades to a corner dot — see the folder icon (Recordings). The count is still announced through the item's aria-label.

This is the same component and the same nav data as the full-width rail — the rail decides what to render from whether its groups carry labels. Nothing in a consuming service changes to get this behaviour beyond giving labeled groups an icon.
